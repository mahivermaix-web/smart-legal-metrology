# References

## Official Government Sources

### 1. Legal Metrology Act, 2009

The Legal Metrology Act, 2009 provides the legislative framework for establishing and enforcing standards of weights and measures and regulating trade and commerce involving goods sold by weight, measure, or number.

**Official source:**
[India Code — Legal Metrology Act, 2009](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/14044?locale=en&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 2. Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

These rules provide requirements relating to pre-packaged commodities and their declarations. The Department of Consumer Affairs lists the original rules and subsequent amendments on its official Legal Metrology page.

**Official source:**
[Department of Consumer Affairs — Legal Metrology Rules and Amendments](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/legal-metrology-act?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 3. Legal Metrology Overview — Department of Consumer Affairs

The Department of Consumer Affairs provides an overview of Legal Metrology and identifies mandatory declarations associated with packaged commodities, including manufacturer/packer/importer details, country of origin where applicable, commodity name, net quantity, date-related information, MRP, consumer-care details, and other declarations.

**Official source:**
[Department of Consumer Affairs — Legal Metrology Overview](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/legal-metrology-overview?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 4. Amendments to the Packaged Commodities Rules

The Department of Consumer Affairs publishes amendments to the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, including amendments issued in 2021 and 2023. The verification rules used by a future implementation of this project should therefore be maintained and updated according to the applicable current regulations.

## Relevance to This Project

These official sources provide the regulatory foundation for the proposed **Smart Legal Metrology Compliance Verification System for Packaged Commodities**.

The project concept is intended to translate applicable requirements into a technology-assisted preliminary verification workflow.

## Important Note

Regulatory requirements may be amended over time. Any future implementation should verify its rules against the latest applicable official notifications, amendments, and guidance before being used for real-world compliance decisions.

The proposed system is intended as a **preliminary verification and decision-support tool**, not as a replacement for official legal or regulatory verification.
